ITSM Data Migration & Quality Governance

Coordination of a multi-domain IT service management migration into Freshservice, with reconciliation and Agile delivery controls.

FreshserviceSQLJiraAgile

Why this work matters

Moving service-management data into a new platform demands correct mappings, accurate migration results, and disciplined coordination across technical and business stakeholders.

My approach

  • Led scope, scheduling and stakeholder communication for a migration spanning five or more data domains.
  • Coordinated engineering and business dependencies, acceptance criteria, and pre-go-live validation.
  • Monitored sprint progress, quality, migration issues and delivery risks using Jira and CloudWatch reporting.
  • Used reconciliation evidence to support cutover quality and readiness decisions.
