Migration Cutover Readiness

A sample review framework for making a go-live decision across data, engineering, business, and operations.

Sample only: Created for this portfolio using fictional checkpoints. This is not an employer document, evidence of a past incident, or a completed project approval.

Decision to support

Can the planned release proceed with agreed acceptance criteria met, accountable owners, and a workable recovery plan? The project manager coordinates the evidence and escalates unresolved risks; authorized business and technical owners approve the release.

Readiness checkpoints

Example escalation

Fictional scenario: Reconciliation shows unexplained differences immediately before the release window. I would make the variance visible, assign an investigation owner, assess the effect on acceptance criteria and dependent work, and bring options to the authorized decision-makers before the cutover deadline.

Proceed with an accepted exception

Only if the variance is understood, within agreed tolerance, and explicitly approved with follow-up ownership.

Defer the affected scope or release

If the discrepancy breaches acceptance criteria or its impact remains unknown, escalate the schedule and dependency consequences for a decision.

Executive update format

Status: Ready / At risk / Blocked
Decision needed: Specific approval or tradeoff, with a deadline
Impact: Scope, schedule, downstream teams, and customer or operational effect
Owner and next checkpoint: Named accountable role and next update time
