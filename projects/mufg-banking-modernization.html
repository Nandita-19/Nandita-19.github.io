Program context

Banking modernization requires coordinated execution across engineering, testing, business ownership, risk and governance. As a Technical Project Manager at MUFG, my focus is on delivery predictability, data quality, stakeholder alignment, and controlled production transitions.

My role and delivery ownership

  • Lead end-to-end delivery planning across Agile and Waterfall teams, managing scope, schedule, budget, milestones, risks, issues, and inter-team dependencies.
  • Coordinate ETL pipeline and migration workstreams involving 200+ pipelines and 10M+ records, aligning data owners on mapping, validation, reconciliation, and readiness.
  • Facilitate stakeholder deep dives, governance reviews and steering updates; maintain PDLC-aligned plans, RAID logs, decision records, and change-control documentation.
  • Coordinate system integration testing (SIT), user acceptance testing (UAT), defect triage, remediation follow-up, go-live acceptance, and cutover activities.
  • Translate technical progress into executive status reporting and delivery KPIs using Jira, Confluence, Excel, and PowerPoint.

How I manage execution risk

Scope & dependencies

Governance & reporting

Keep PDLC artifacts, RAID entries, decisions, budget/schedule changes, and stakeholder follow-up current.

Production readiness

Coordinate cutover planning, issue ownership, acceptance checkpoints, and support handoff.
