Power BI Sales Analytics

A practical analytics learning exercise exploring tabular data, Power Query, DAX measures, report views and reporting conventions.

Power BIPower QueryDAXData modeling

Why this work matters

Sales data in spreadsheets can be difficult to interpret without consistent measures and reusable data relationships. This exercise is a learning sandbox for creating clear, repeatable business reporting.

My approach

  • Prepared a small example sales dataset with orders, products, category, quantity, sales and profit fields.
  • Practiced the role of data sources, Power Query, semantic models and DAX in the Power BI workflow.
  • Worked with a Total Sales measure and explored the report, data and model views.
  • Continue developing validation, measures and visuals as hands-on exercises.

Planned demonstration checklist

Model quality

DAX measures

Show transparent definitions for Total Sales, Profit, and Margin, with a basic validation table.

Report UX
